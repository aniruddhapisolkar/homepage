## Combine moves

- **Unreachable state.** Identify the legal moves, choose a candidate invariant, and verify it for each move. Compare its start and target values.
- **Optimal answer.** Construct a candidate, derive a bound for every allowed choice, and check whether the candidate attains it.
- **Suspected pattern.** Test small cases and edge cases, state an exact claim, and try to prove it. Change the claim if the evidence demands it.
- **Uncertain prediction.** Write down the model's assumptions, estimate a range, and name an observation that could separate competing explanations.

## Pick a study path

- **Starting from scratch.** Follow the chapter order through Part I, then work through Part II. Do each part's checkpoint before moving ahead.
- **Preparing for contest problems.** After Part I, focus on Chapters [7](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-07/), [8](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-08/), [10](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-10/), [11](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-11/), [12](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-12/), [13](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-13/), [17](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-17/), and 24. Mix old and new problems without labeling the expected method.
- **Applying math to data or computing.** After Part I, review Chapters [7](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-07/), [12](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-12/), [15](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-15/), [21](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-21/), and 22. Work through Part IV, then build and test a model for a question you can measure.
- **Starting an investigation.** After Part III, try Chapters [31](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-31/)–[35](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-35/) in order. Keep a log with one question, your evidence, your strongest claim, and the next test.
