## Part III checkpoint

Find the largest product of two positive integers whose sum is 17. Prove your result with an exchange argument and then with a bound. Change the sum to an arbitrary integer $n\ge2$ and explain how the best pair depends on whether $n$ is odd or even. Compare what each proof reveals about equality.

### Further challenge

Prove the coloring claim from Chapter [21](/homepage/portfolio/tsmt/chapter-21/) without using chance. Pick a coloring with as many crossing edges as possible. What would happen if you changed one vertex's color? Show that each vertex must have at least half its neighbors in the other color, then count the ends of the crossing edges. Compare the local guarantee you get here with the existence argument from probability.
